# Lab Safety and Responsible Use

This repository is intended for authorized education and personal homelab use.

- Do not expose DVWA or the lab Linux services to the public Internet.
- Use host-only or otherwise isolated virtual networks.
- Do not reuse production credentials, cookies, or data.
- Do not copy employer configurations or logs into this repository.
- Use only harmless test files and targets you own.
- Disable temporary Telnet, FTP, NAT, and broad firewall rules after validation.
- Take snapshots before testing and reset DVWA after destructive exercises.
- Review every screenshot before publishing for private bookmarks, email addresses, tokens, or personal paths.

The payloads and commands in this repository are scoped to a deliberately vulnerable local DVWA instance and private RFC1918 lab addresses.
